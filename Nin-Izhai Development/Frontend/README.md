# Nin Izhai (நின் இழை)

*Every thread, for you.*

*Nin* is the classical Tamil word for "your", and *izhai* is a single fine strand of thread.

## About

Nin Izhai is an order platform for aari-work blouses.

Customers can browse a catalogue of designs, choose the type of work they want, share their measurements and place an order. Each order comes with a price and an estimated delivery date, and customers can track it until it's ready.
